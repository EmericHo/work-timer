France's fast charging infrastructure has experienced exponential growth in 2025-2026. With more than 12,000 fast charging points (>50 kW) deployed across the territory, charging your electric vehicle while traveling has never been easier. In this guide, I compare the main networks, their rates, their coverage, and give you my advice to optimize your charging.

## 2026 State of Play: Charging Figures in France

### National statistics

- **Total charging points**: 142,000 (all types combined)
- **Fast DC charge > 50 kW**: 12,400 points
- **Ultra-fast charge > 150 kW**: 3,800 points
- **Highway stations**: 580 equipped rest areas (100% of network)
- **Average availability**: 94.2% (uptime)

### 2023-2026 Growth

The number of fast chargers has **tripled** in 3 years. Massive investments from operators (Ionity, Fastned, Total Energies) and the France Relance plan enabled this acceleration.

## Major Fast Charging Networks

### Ionity: European Premium

**Characteristics**:
- Stations: 85 in France (highway network exclusively)
- Power: 350 kW per charger (800V ready)
- Chargers/station: 6 to 12 HPC chargers
- Connector: CCS Combo 2 (European standard)

**2026 Rates**:
- **Without subscription**: €0.69/kWh
- **With Ionity Passport subscription (€13/month)**: €0.35/kWh
- **Via manufacturer subscription**: €0.30/kWh (Mercedes, BMW, Audi, Porsche, Hyundai)

**Coverage**: Highway axes A1, A6, A7, A10, A63. Every 150 km maximum.

**My opinion**: Ionity is the most reliable and powerful network. Stations are well maintained, rarely saturated. The rate without subscription is prohibitive, but with Passport or manufacturer subscription, it's competitive. **Recommended for high-mileage drivers.**

### Tesla Supercharger: Historic Network Opened

**Characteristics**:
- Stations: 180 in France
- Power: 150 to 250 kW (V3 and V4)
- Chargers/station: 8 to 20 chargers
- Connector: CCS Combo 2 (V3/V4 stations)

**2026 Rates**:
- **Tesla owner**: €0.37/kWh (peak hours) / €0.28/kWh (off-peak hours)
- **Non-Tesla (access via app)**: €0.52/kWh / €0.42/kWh (with €12/month subscription: €0.45 / €0.35)

**Coverage**: Highway + suburban. Densest network in the French market.

**My opinion**: Opening to non-Tesla in 2024 = game changer. Legendary reliability (97% availability rate), dense network. Competitive Tesla rate, a bit expensive for non-Tesla. **Best choice for Tesla owners, excellent backup for others.**

### Fastned: Premium Experience

**Characteristics**:
- Stations: 52 in France (highway + national roads)
- Power: 300 kW per charger
- Chargers/station: 4 to 8 chargers
- Connector: CCS Combo 2 + CHAdeMO

**2026 Rates**:
- **Without subscription**: €0.65/kWh
- **Gold subscription (€12/month)**: €0.42/kWh
- **Gold+ (€25/month)**: €0.35/kWh (+ partner stations)

**Coverage**: Mainly North/North-East (A1, A4, A26). Southern expansion underway.

**My opinion**: Design stations (recognizable yellow architecture), solar roof, premium experience. Network still geographically limited but rapid expansion. **Recommended if your trips cover their network.**

### Total Energies: The Versatile

**Characteristics**:
- Stations: 320 in France (highway + urban)
- Power: 50 to 175 kW
- Chargers/station: 2 to 4 chargers
- Connector: CCS Combo 2

**2026 Rates**:
- **Without subscription**: €0.55/kWh
- **With TotalEnergies Charge card (free)**: €0.48/kWh
- **Premium formula (€9/month)**: €0.39/kWh

**Coverage**: The widest. Highway network + urban service stations. 3,200 AC/DC charging points.

**My opinion**: Most geographically accessible network. Decent but not exceptional power. Average rates. **Ideal as secondary/backup network.**

### Electra: Urban Challenger

**Characteristics**:
- Stations: 180 in France (urban/suburban)
- Power: 150 to 300 kW
- Chargers/station: 4 to 12 chargers
- Connector: CCS Combo 2

**2026 Rates**:
- **Without subscription**: €0.49/kWh
- **Easy subscription (€8/month)**: €0.39/kWh
- **Business (on quote)**: negotiated rate

**Coverage**: Shopping centers, urban parking. No highways.

**My opinion**: Excellent for charging while shopping. Recent, reliable stations. Not suitable for long highway trips. **Recommended for urban/suburban use.**

## Subscription Comparison: Which Network to Choose?

### Occasional use (< 500 kWh/year)

**No subscription**. Favor Total Energies (€0.48/kWh with free card) and Electra (€0.49/kWh).

**Estimated annual cost**: €240 for 500 kWh

### Regular use (500-2000 kWh/year)

**Ionity Passport (€13/month) + Tesla Supercharger (€12/month)** = €25/month

Charge 1500 kWh/year:
- 50% Ionity: 750 kWh × €0.35 = €262
- 50% Tesla: 750 kWh × €0.45 = €337
- Subscriptions: €300
- **Total: €899** (vs €735 without subscription on Total at €0.49)

**Verdict**: Profitable if you value speed (charge 2x faster than Total) and availability.

### Intensive use (> 2000 kWh/year)

**Ionity Passport + Fastned Gold+ + Tesla Supercharger** = €50/month

Charge 3000 kWh/year:

vs without subscription Total: 3000 × €0.48 = €1,440

**Verdict**: Multiple subscriptions not profitable for pure charging. Interest = availability + speed + national coverage.

## Tips to Optimize Your Charging

### Highway Roaming

1. **Plan ahead**: Use ABRP before departure. Identify 2 options per stop (backup if station saturated).

2. **Charge 20-80%**: It's the optimal range. Charging from 80% to 100% takes 2x longer for 20% range.

3. **Avoid peak hours**: Saturday 10am-12pm and 3pm-5pm = saturation. Favor 7am-9am or after 7pm.

4. **Connect before paying**: Check that the charger works. On Ionity/Fastned, plug in first, payment starts automatically.

5. **Monitor charging**: The first 10 minutes are crucial. If power < 50 kW, unplug and change charger.

### Urban

1. **Charge while shopping**: Electra, Engie, Lidl offer chargers in shopping centers. 30-45 min = 200 km recovered.

2. **Take advantage of off-peak hours**: Some operators (Total) apply reduced rates 10pm-6am.

3. **Avoid public AC charging**: 22 kW × €0.35/kWh in public parking = more expensive than fast charge. Prefer home charging when possible.

## Home Charger Installation: The Real Savings

**Average cost**: €1,200-1,800 installation included (7 kW single-phase charger)

**Electricity rate**: €0.18/kWh (off-peak hours) vs €0.45/kWh public

**Profitability**: From 8,000 kWh charged (about 50,000 km), installation is amortized.

**Incentives**: 75% tax credit (capped €300), ADVENIR bonus €500 (collective).

**Advice**: If you have garage/private parking, home charger = investment #1. Save 40-60% vs public charging.
