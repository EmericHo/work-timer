Electric car prices have fallen considerably between 2020 and 2026, with the arrival of ultra-competitive Chinese manufacturers and optimization of battery production costs. In this 2026 buying guide, I select the best value for money by segment, taking into account the eco bonus, real range, and operating costs.

## 2026 Eco Bonus Impact

### Current Scale

**Environmental score > 60** (mainly made in Europe):
- Vehicle < €47,000: **€4,000** bonus
- Vehicle €47,000-67,000: **€2,000** bonus
- Vehicle > €67,000: **€0** bonus

**Score < 60** (imported China, USA):
- Vehicle < €47,000: **€1,500** bonus
- Vehicle > €47,000: **€0** bonus

### Additional Conversion Bonus

If replacing diesel vehicle > 15 years or gasoline > 20 years:
- Tax income < €15,400/share: **€2,500**
- Income €15,400-24,300: **€1,500**

**Possible combination**: Bonus + conversion bonus = up to **€6,500** aid.

## Traps to Avoid When Buying

### 1. Overestimated Range

**WLTP ≠ real**. Count -15 to -25% depending on use (highway worst case).

### 2. Plethora of Options

Metallic paint +€600, 19" wheels +€800, pano roof +€1200... Options inflate bill quickly.

**Advice**: Stay on intermediate finish. Options don't resell.

### 3. Apparent Cheap Leasing

"€199/month!" with €8,000 down payment + €500 file fee + limited km. Read the fine print.

### 4. Too Recent Used

A 2024 electric at -20% vs new? Check transferable battery warranty + reason for quick sale (hidden problem?).

### 5. Unknown Brand Without Network

MG, Xpeng, BYD have < 100 service points France. If close to you OK, otherwise maintenance hassle.
