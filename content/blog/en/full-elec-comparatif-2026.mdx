## BYD Seal: Chinese value for money

### Design and positioning

The BYD Seal is a sports sedan that directly targets the Tesla Model 3. Fluid design, aerodynamic coefficient of 0.27, the Seal doesn't lack allure with its taut lines and clean front face.

### Strengths ✅

**Unbeatable price**: At €38,990 after bonus, it's the cheapest electric in this comparison with real premium features.

**Excellent range**: 480 km real in mixed use, 550 km on stabilized road. BYD's LFP (lithium-iron-phosphate) battery is renowned for its longevity.

**Delightful performance**: 313 hp for 5.9s 0-100 km/h. Acceleration is strong, chassis well-balanced.

**Blade battery technology**: BYD's famous Blade battery offers superior safety (resists nail penetration test without catching fire) and 3000 guaranteed full charge cycles.

**Careful finish**: Modern cabin with 15.6" rotating screen, leather upholstery, panoramic roof as standard. Decent assembly quality.

### Weaknesses ❌

**Relatively slow charging**: 37 minutes on fast charge. The 400V architecture limits power to 150 kW max.

**Limited service network**: BYD is developing its network, but only 80 service points in France vs 180 for Renault.

**Resale unknown**: First generation, no track record on depreciation. Risk of strong depreciation.

**Perfectible infotainment**: Interface in French, but navigation less intuitive than Tesla or Xpeng. OTA updates announced but infrequent to date.

**Modest trunk**: 400L, that's 100L less than the segment average.

## Xpeng G6: The technological rocket

### The SUV of the future

The Xpeng G6 is the first democratized 800V electric SUV. Advanced electrical architecture, LiDAR for autonomous driving, the G6 pushes technological boundaries.

### Strengths ✅

**Ultra-fast charging**: Thanks to the 800V architecture, the G6 charges at 300 kW peak. 10-80% in only 26 minutes, absolute record of this comparison.

**Leading range**: 510 km real. 87 kWh NMC battery coupled with remarkable efficiency (16.2 kWh/100).

**Advanced XNGP**: The autonomous driving system with dual LiDAR offers impressive level 2+ semi-autonomous driving. Works without mapping on 95% of the French network.

**Sports car performance**: 400 hp, 0-100 in 5.9s. All-wheel drive ensures perfect traction.

**XXL trunk**: 571L + 71L frunk. Real family capacity.

**Powerful OTA**: Monthly updates that actually add features. The G6 improves over time.

### Weaknesses ❌

**Slightly higher price**: €40,990 after bonus. €2,000 more than BYD, justified by the tech.

**Still young network**: Xpeng has 45 service points. Development underway but less dense network than established manufacturers.

**Specific consumables**: 20" tires expensive (€800 rear axle), specific brake pads.

**Resale unknown**: Newcomer, no depreciation history. Bet on the brand's future in Europe.

## Tesla Model Y: The aging reference

### The contested bestseller

The Model Y remains the best-selling electric car in the world. But faced with 2026 competition, does it maintain its lead?

### Strengths ✅

**Supercharger network**: 450 stations in France, +2000 chargers. Fast charging guaranteed everywhere. Also access to third-party chargers since 2024.

**Charging efficiency**: 27 minutes 10-80%. 400V architecture, but remarkable software optimization.

**Performance**: Long Range Dual Motor version: 513 hp, 0-100 in 5.0s. It's a real bomb.

**Mature Autopilot**: The Tesla system remains a reference in level 2 autonomous driving. FSD available as option (€89/month).

**Gigantic trunk**: 854L + 117L frunk. Unbeatable loading volume.

**OTA updates**: Tesla inventor of the concept. Regular new features (games, range improvement, new aids).

**Insurance**: Excellent residual value (60% at 3 years). Maximum liquidity at resale.

### Weaknesses ❌

**Rising price**: €42,990 after bonus. Tesla increased its prices in early 2026 (+€3000).

**Perfectible finish**: Variable assembly quality, irregular gaps. No real improvement vs previous generations.

**Austere cabin**: Minimalist design that divides. No instrument cluster, everything on 15" central screen. Unconventional ergonomics.

**Range falling behind**: 465 km real, that's 45 km less than the G6, 75 km less than the Scenic.

**High consumption**: 17.1 kWh/100, the highest in the comparison. Weight of 1980 kg + less worked aerodynamics than 2026 competition.

**Tesla service**: Reputed complicated, sometimes long intervention delays, expensive spare parts.

## FAQ: Your questions about 2026 electrics

**Are Chinese batteries reliable?**

BYD and CATL (Xpeng supplier) are the two largest producers worldwide. Their NMC and LFP batteries have proven themselves. BYD guarantees 3000 cycles on its Blade, or 1,000,000 km theoretical.

**Does fast charging damage the battery?**

Modern chemistries (NMC 811, LFP) tolerate occasional fast charging well. Just avoid charging to 100% fast + leaving at 100% several days. BMS (Battery Management Systems) protect effectively.

**Can I install a 22 kW charger at home?**

All these models accept 11 kW in AC (3 phases). Sufficient for a complete overnight charge. Going to 22 kW requires a specific onboard charger (optional) + reinforced electrical installation.

**What is the real lifespan of an EV battery?**

2026 data shows average degradation of 2-3% per year the first 5 years, then slowing to 1%/year. After 10 years, expect to retain 75-80% of original capacity. Widely usable.

**Is 800V really useful?**

On the highway, yes. The Xpeng G6 charges 2 times faster than others. On a Paris-Marseille, that represents 30 minutes saved. In daily urban use, the interest is nil (overnight charging sufficient).
