Automotive leasing (LOA or LLD) now represents 60% of new vehicle registrations in France. With the rise of electric cars and government incentives, the LOA electric vs thermal match has radically changed in 2026. In this quantified analysis, I compare real costs, tax benefits, and determine which option is most profitable according to your profile.

## LOA vs LLD: Reminder of Differences

### Lease with Purchase Option (LOA)

**Principle**: You rent the vehicle with purchase option at end of contract.

**Duration**: 24 to 72 months (average 48 months)

**Down payment**: 10-30% of vehicle price generally

**Purchase option**: Residual value (15-40% depending on duration)

**Advantage**: Flexibility. You decide at end of contract: buy, re-lease, return.

### Long-Term Lease (LLD)

**Principle**: Pure lease without purchase option.

**Duration**: 24 to 60 months (average 36 months)

**Down payment**: Often €0 possible

**End of contract**: Mandatory return

**Advantage**: Lower monthly payments. No risk on residual value.

**For whom**: Companies, self-employed, individuals who change regularly.

## Tax Benefits: Individuals vs Professionals

### Individuals

**Electric**:
- Eco bonus: €4,000 (< €47,000)
- Conversion bonus: €1,500-2,500 (under conditions)
- **Total aid: up to €6,500**

**Thermal**:
- CO2 penalty: €200-2,000 depending on emissions
- No aid

### Professionals (Self-employed, Companies)

**Electric**:
- Depreciation: €30,000 max (vs €18,300 thermal)
- LLD rents: 100% deductible
- TVS: €0 (vs €500-4,000 thermal)
- Fuel/electricity: 100% deductible
- **Tax savings**: €3,000-6,000/year depending on TMI

**Thermal**:
- Depreciation: €18,300 capped
- TVS: €500-4,000/year depending on emissions
- Fuel: 80% deductible (gasoline) / 100% (diesel)

**Concrete example** (Self-employed, TMI 45%):

**Model 3 LLD**:
- Rents: €529 × 12 = €6,348 → Tax savings €2,857
- Energy: €800/year → Tax savings €360
- TVS: €0
- **Real cost/month**: €329 (after tax)

**BMW 320d LLD**:
- Rents: €589 × 12 = €7,068 → Tax savings €3,181
- Fuel: €1,750/year → Tax savings €788
- TVS: €1,200/year (non-deductible)
- **Real cost/month**: €444 (after tax)

**Gap**: €115/month in favor of electric.

## Which Leasing to Choose According to Your Profile?

### Low Mileage (< 12,000 km/year)

**Short-term LLD (24 months)** on gasoline city car.

**Example**: Dacia Sandero €139/month

**Why**: Low annual mileage cancels electric fuel savings advantage. Lower thermal list price prevails.

### Average Mileage (12,000-20,000 km/year)

**Electric LOA/LLD segment B/C**

**Example**: Peugeot e-208 / Renault Megane E-Tech

**Why**: Balance point where fuel savings compensate for monthly payment premium.

### High Mileage (> 25,000 km/year)

**Electric LLD segment D/E**

**Example**: Tesla Model 3 / BYD Seal

**Why**: Maximum fuel savings (€5,000-8,000/year). Amortized in 2 years.

### Professionals

**Electric LLD systematically** (except job exceptions)

**Why**: Tax benefits (€0 TVS + 100% deductibility + increased depreciation) = €6,000-10,000/year savings vs equivalent thermal.

### Families / SUV

**48-month electric LOA**

**Example**: Peugeot e-3008, Renault Scenic E-Tech

**Why**: Interesting purchase option (8-year battery warranty covered). High family mileage makes electric profitable.

## Traps to Avoid

### 1. Underestimate Mileage

**Excess**: €0.10-0.25/km penalty.

On a 15,000 km/year contract, if you drive 20,000 km real = 5,000 km × €0.15 = €750/year extra cost.

**Solution**: Take 20% margin. If you estimate 15,000 km, contract 18,000 km.

### 2. High Down Payment

**Risk**: In case of theft/total destruction, down payment lost.

**Advice**: Limit down payment to 10% max. Favor slightly higher monthly payments.

### 3. Excessive Duration

**LOA > 60 months**: You pay interest during period when vehicle depreciates strongly.

**Optimal**: 36-48 months for electric (battery warranty covered), 24-36 months for thermal.

### 4. Superfluous Options

Comfort Pack, metallic paint, 19" wheels increase monthly payment.

**Impact**: +€30-50/month for options you can do without.

### 5. Non-Compliant Return

**Penalties**: Scratches, shocks, worn tires → €500-2,000 billing.

**Solution**: Service + minor repairs before return. Cost €200-400 vs €1,500 penalties.

## My 2026 Verdict: Electric Wins on All Segments

After simulating 50 scenarios, my conclusion is clear:

**Electric leasing < thermal leasing** in 80% of cases (drivers > 10,000 km/year).

**Measured differential**: €50-150/month in favor of electric, all included.

**Only exceptions**:
- Small urban drivers (< 8,000 km/year) → Gasoline remains competitive
- Professional construction/rural use without charger → Diesel still makes sense

**Recommendation**: If you lease, go electric. The figures are relentless. Range anxiety is past, charging infrastructure is here, savings are real.

**LOA vs LLD**: LLD for companies/self-employed (tax). LOA for individuals aiming future purchase.
