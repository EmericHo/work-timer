## Peugeot e-3008: The game-changing electric

### The real range figures

With its 81 kWh battery, the e-3008 displays 480 km WLTP range. In real-world mixed use conditions, expect rather 420-450 km per charge, which remains excellent for an electric SUV of this size.

### Electric advantages ✅

**Savings in use**: At €0.18/kWh during off-peak hours, the cost per 100 km is €2.88. Over 100,000 km, you save more than €8,000 compared to gasoline.

**Advantageous taxation**: The €4,000 eco bonus + the absence of penalty make the final purchase price very competitive at €44,500.

**Performance and silence**: The 210 hp available instantly offer strong acceleration, all in remarkable operating silence.

**Reduced maintenance**: No oil changes, no clutch, no timing belt. Preventive maintenance is limited to brakes (moderately stressed thanks to regeneration), tires and filters.

### Electric disadvantages ❌

**Charging time**: Even with a 150 kW fast charger, expect 28 minutes to go from 20% to 80%. On the highway, this requires regular stops on long trips.

**High list price**: Despite the bonus, the initial investment remains €8,000 higher than gasoline before incentives.

**Charging infrastructure**: If you don't have a home charger, public charging can be restrictive and more expensive (€0.45-0.65/kWh).

**Battery depreciation**: Although the warranty covers 8 years/160,000 km, capacity loss (about 5-8% over 5 years) can be concerning.

## 3008 Plug-in Hybrid: The versatile compromise

### The hybrid promise

The system combines a 1.6 PureTech gasoline engine of 150 hp and an 81 kW (110 hp) electric motor for a combined power of 225 hp. The 21 kWh battery offers 85 km WLTP electric range (65-70 km real).

### Hybrid advantages ✅

**Total flexibility**: Electric in the city, thermal on the highway. No range anxiety for long trips.

**Efficient electric mode**: 85 km is enough for 90% of daily trips. Rechargeable in 2h on a reinforced domestic socket (3.7 kW).

**Comfortable power**: The combined 225 hp offers superior performance to the other two versions (0-100 km/h in 8.9s).

**Access to incentives**: The €1,000 bonus is not negligible, and some regions add local incentives.

### Hybrid disadvantages ❌

**High total cost of ownership**: The purchase price (€46,200 after bonus) + the dual powertrain maintenance costs increase the TCO to €12,800 over 100,000 km.

**Mechanical complexity**: Two propulsion systems = two potential sources of failure. Long-term reliability remains to be proven (little feedback > 150,000 km).

**Battery degradation**: PHEV batteries, more stressed in charge/discharge cycles, can lose 20-30% capacity after 100,000 km.

**Penalizing weight**: The 1,850 kg affects thermal consumption. In gasoline-only mode, expect 8-9 L/100 km.

**Conditional profitability**: Without daily recharging, you're driving a gasoline vehicle 200 kg heavier. The purchase premium is never amortized.

## 3008 PureTech 136: Affordable gasoline... with reservations

### The divisive engine

The 1.2 PureTech 3-cylinder turbo develops 136 hp. It's the best-selling engine in the range, but also the most controversial.

### Gasoline advantages ✅

**Purchase price**: At €41,813 after penalty, it's €2,700 less than electric and €4,400 less than hybrid.

**Mechanical simplicity**: A single well-known propulsion system, universal repair network.

**Infinite distribution network**: No planning necessary, gas stations everywhere.

**Easier resale**: Mature used market, maximum liquidity.

### Major gasoline disadvantages ❌

**Eco penalty**: €1,513 in 2026, continuously increasing (€2,000+ expected in 2027).

**Prohibitive operating cost**: At €1.85/L average 2026 and 7.8 L/100 km real, fuel represents €14.43 per 100 km, or €14,430 over 100,000 km.

**PureTech reliability**: Wet timing belt problems are documented. Although Stellantis has extended the warranty (SToA 10 years), monitoring remains necessary.

**Modest performance**: 136 hp is sufficient for everyday use, but acceleration is less sharp than on electric or hybrid.

**Accelerated depreciation**: With tightening standards, gasoline vehicles are losing their attractiveness. Residual value of only 45% at 3 years.

## Which choice for which user profile?

### Choose the electric e-3008 if:

- You travel less than 400 km/day
- You have access to home or workplace charging
- Your budget allows the initial investment (with bonus)
- You're looking for minimum operating costs
- You're keeping the vehicle > 5 years

**Typical profile**: Active person with garage, commute < 100 km, regular weekend regional use, sensitive to long-term savings.

### Choose the hybrid if:

- You regularly make trips > 500 km
- You can recharge daily (essential!)
- You value maximum versatility
- Your urban use dominates (> 70% of mileage)

**Typical profile**: Sales rep, field commercial, family making city-countryside round trips, need for absolute flexibility.

### Choose gasoline only if:

- Your purchase budget is very constrained
- You drive few kilometers (< 15,000 km/year)
- You're reselling quickly (< 3 years)
- You have no possibility of recharging

**Typical profile**: Occasional driver, secondary use, quick resale planned.

## Verdict and Veldra recommendation

**My personal choice**: The Peugeot e-3008 stands out as the best quality-price-use ratio for the majority of users in 2026. The real range of 450 km covers 95% of needs, and the savings over 5 years (€7,400 vs gasoline) are substantial.

**Hybrid exception**: If your trips regularly exceed 600 km and you religiously recharge every night, the hybrid remains relevant.

## FAQ: Your questions about the 3008 comparison

**Is electric range sufficient in winter?**

Expect a loss of 20-25% in winter conditions (< 5°C, active heating). The e-3008 then drops to 350-380 km real. Still widely usable on a daily basis.

**Is the PureTech reliable now?**

Post-2023 versions incorporate improvements (reinforced belt). Stellantis offers an extended 10-year/200,000 km warranty on the timing system. Caution recommended, strict maintenance compliance mandatory.

**Can I finance the electric in lease/LLD?**

Yes, manufacturers offer attractive offers: ~€350/month for the e-3008 over 48 months / 40,000 km, battery warranty included. Compare with gasoline at ~€280/month + €240 monthly fuel.

**Does the hybrid require two services?**

No, a single annual service, but more complex (dual system verification). Cost increased by 30-40% vs pure thermal gasoline.
