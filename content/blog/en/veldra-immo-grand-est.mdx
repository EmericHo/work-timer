## Recommended Investment Strategies

After 8 years' experience in property and crypto investment, here are my proven strategies for the Grand Est:

### Strategy 1: Multi 1-Bedroom Student Properties
Target university cities (Strasbourg, Nancy, Reims) with 1-bedroom flats near campuses. Low tenant turnover if well located, high demand.

**Advantages**:
- Yields 6-7%
- Strong and constant demand
- Simplified management

**Disadvantages**:
- Higher turnover
- Potential damage
- Seasonality (9-10 month tenancies)

### Strategy 2: Family 2-Bedroom Suburban Property
Invest in 2-3 bedroom flats on the outskirts of major cities, targeting families.

**Advantages**:
- Stable tenants (3-5 years)
- Good property maintenance
- Optimised taxation (deductible charges)

**Disadvantages**:
- Slightly lower yields (5-6%)
- Higher investment amount

### Strategy 3: Luxembourg Cross-Border
Invest in Thionville, Longwy or surrounding areas for Luxembourg workers.

**Advantages**:
- High rents (Luxembourg income levels)
- Very strong demand
- Creditworthy tenants
- Yields 7-8%

**Disadvantages**:
- Increased competition
- Rising prices
- Dependence on Luxembourg market

## Rental Taxation 2026: What's Changed

The 2026 tax reform has modified several aspects of rental taxation:

### Micro-foncier Regime (income < €15,000/year)
- Standard allowance: 30%
- Taxation on 70% of rent
- Simple but often less advantageous

### Real Costs Regime (Régime Réel)
- Deduction of all actual charges
- Depreciation of furnishings (furnished lettings)
- Property deficit deductible (€10,700/year)
- **Recommended** for tax optimisation

### Profitability Calculation Methodology
The yield calculations presented follow the standard methodology:
- **Gross yield** = (Annual rent / Total purchase price) × 100
- **Net yield** = (Annual rent - Non-recoverable expenses) / Total purchase price × 100
- **Cash flow** = Rental income - (Loan payment + Expenses + Taxes)
