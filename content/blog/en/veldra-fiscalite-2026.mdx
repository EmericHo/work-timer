The 2026 tax reform on property income represents the most significant change since 2018. With over 3.5 million landlords affected in France, understanding these new rules is crucial for optimising your property investment strategy. Here is my comprehensive analysis following in-depth study of the legislative texts.

## The 5 Major Changes of the 2026 Reform

### 1. Modification of the Property Deficit Cap

**Before 2026**: Deduction limited to €10,700/year on global income
**From 2026**: New cap of €15,000/year

This 40% increase is excellent news for investors carrying out major works. Excess deficit remains carried forward for 10 years on future property income.

**Concrete example**:
- Property income: €12,000
- Deductible works: €30,000
- Deficit: -€18,000
- Deduction on global income: -€15,000 (instead of -€10,700 previously)
- Carried forward to future years: -€3,000

### 2. New Intermediate Regime

Between micro-foncier and standard regime, an intermediate regime appears for income between €15,000 and €30,000.

**Characteristics**:
- Standard allowance: 40% (instead of 30% in micro-foncier)
- Simplified declaration (no detailed receipts)
- 3-year option (renewable)

**Simulation with our calculator**:
- Annual rent: €24,000
- Micro regime (30%): Taxation on €16,800
- Intermediate regime (40%): Taxation on €14,400
- **Saving**: ~€720/year (30% marginal rate)

### 3. Modification of Loan Interest Calculation

Loan interest remains deductible, but with a new cap for loans contracted from 2026.

**New rules**:
- Effective rate > 5%: Deduction capped at 5% of remaining capital
- Effective rate ≤ 5%: Full deduction maintained

This measure aims to limit aggressive tax schemes whilst protecting "normal" investors benefiting from market rates.

### 4. Strengthening of LMNP (Non-Professional Furnished Landlord)

LMNP status becomes even more attractive in 2026 with several improvements:

**2026 LMNP Updates**:
- Micro-BIC threshold: Increase to €77,700 (instead of €72,600)
- Standard allowance: Maintained at 50%
- Property depreciation: Extended to 40 years (instead of 30 years)
- Combined LMNP + unfurnished rental: Authorised under conditions

**Unfurnished vs furnished rental comparison**:

| Criterion | Unfurnished rental | Furnished rental |
|---------|--------------|------------------|
| Taxation | Property income | BIC |
| Micro allowance | 30% | 50% |
| Depreciation | No | Yes |
| Deductible charges | Limited | Extended |
| Deficit | €15,000/year | Full amount |

### 5. Relaxation of SCI (Civil Real Estate Company) Regime

SCIs benefit from new tax flexibilities in 2026:

**SCI Updates**:
- IR → IS transition facilitated (relaxed conditions)
- Accelerated first-year depreciation (20% instead of 10%)
- Dividend distribution: Flat tax at 28% (instead of 30%)
- Share transfer: Enhanced allowance for long-term holding

## Special Case: Energy Renovation

The 2026 reform drastically strengthens incentives for energy renovation:

**New benefits**:
- Enhanced deduction: 150% of energy renovation works
- Specific cap: €25,000/property (independent of standard property deficit)
- Cumulative tax credit: Yes (under conditions)

**Insulation works example**:
- Actual cost: €20,000
- Tax deduction: €30,000 (150%)
- 41% marginal rate: Saving of €12,300
- Renovation tax credit: €4,000
- **Total benefit**: €16,300 for €20,000 of works

## 2026 Optimisation Strategies

### Strategy 1: Works Planning

**Objective**: Maximise use of property deficit

**Method**:
- Year N: Works up to €15,000 deficit
- Year N+1: New works if necessary
- Carry forward surplus over 10 years

**Example**:
- 2026: Works €40,000 → Deficit €25,000 → Deduction €15,000 + Carry forward €10,000
- 2027: No works → Deduction of carry forward (if insufficient property income)

### Strategy 2: Unfurnished / Furnished Rental Arbitrage

With the strengthening of LMNP, converting certain properties to furnished may be wise.

**Decision criteria**:
- Rent > €30,000: LMNP often optimal
- High-demand area: Furnished often more profitable
- Target students/mobility: Furnished preferable

### Strategy 3: SCI Optimisation

For substantial estates, SCI under IS may now become attractive:

**IS advantages**:
- IS at 15% up to €42,500 profits
- IS at 25% beyond
- Full property depreciation
- Unlimited deficit carry forward

**IS disadvantages**:
- Capital gains taxed on exit
- Distributions subject to flat tax
- Heavier accounting management

## Mistakes to Avoid in 2026

### Mistake 1: Staying in micro-foncier by habit
The new intermediate regime may be more advantageous. Do the calculation.

### Mistake 2: Not optimising works timing
Plan your works to maximise property deficit deduction at €15,000/year.

### Mistake 3: Neglecting energy renovation
Tax benefits are massive. EPC F/G → C/D can be almost free with assistance.

### Mistake 4: Poorly documenting charges
Under standard regime, every receipt counts. Keep EVERYTHING (invoices, statements, certificates).

## 2026 Tax Calendar to Remember

- **Before 31 March**: Option for standard or intermediate regime
- **April-June**: 2025 property income declaration
- **September**: Tax notice
- **October**: Adjusted withholding tax
